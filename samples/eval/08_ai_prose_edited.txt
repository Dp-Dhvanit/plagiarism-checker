In today's changing cities, heat building up in built-up areas has become a big
challenge for planners. This issue covers several connected things, from how infrastructure is
designed to what happens to public health.

Research shows that hard surfaces raise local temperatures. Cutting back on plants and trees makes
this worse, which creates a cycle that hits vulnerable people hardest.

New solutions are needed to build tougher communities. Cities should use modern technology and take
a broad approach to make mitigation work better. Spending early on green infrastructure is a real
opportunity.

To sum up, dealing with urban heat needs a strong, many-sided approach. Going ahead, everyone
involved has to work together well to get lasting results for the people who come after us.
